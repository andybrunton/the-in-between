Skin buckling between two joints — compression drives outward bulge with an inward crease at a controllable point.

Inputs
Two joint positions, bend angle, bulge amount, crease depth, crease offset along span
Output
Skin curve points (peak + crease profile) — drive a curve, ribbon, or blendshape target

As the joint angle closes, geometric compression grows via a cosine term. That compression inflates the skin outward, then a crease subtracts depth at a chosen point along the span — giving you the classic neck or elbow fold.

Pseudocode 
compression = restSpan * (1 - cos(angle / 2))
bulge = compression * bulgeAmount
crease = bulge * creaseDepth
peak = lerp(attachA, attachB, creaseOffset)
height = bulge - crease * crease_profile(u)
skin_curve = sample_along_span(height)
