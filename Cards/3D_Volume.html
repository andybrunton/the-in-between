Per-axis ellipsoid bulge at a joint — each axis swells independently as angle enters its active range.

Inputs
Joint angle, per-axis min/max angle ranges, base radius, max bulge per axis, easing mode
Output
Ellipsoid radii (Rx, Ry, Rz) or scale vector — drive any volume primitive or squash/stretch

Map joint angle to 0–1 per axis inside its window, ease it, then scale each radius from a rest value. Axes can activate at different angles so volume shifts realistically as the limb bends.

Pseudocode 
for each axis i in [X, Y, Z]:
  t = clamp((angle - min_i) / (max_i - min_i), 0, 1)
  progress_i = ease(t, mode)
  R_i = r0 * (1 + maxBulge_i * progress_i)
// apply R as ellipsoid radii or joint scale
